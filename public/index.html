BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few good
questions.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Choose a shared twin room and we can pair you with another guest, in separate beds. You don't need to book a private room simply because you are travelling alone.

How fit do I need to be?

This is a road cycling camp with climbing. As a guide, aim to be comfortable riding 50 km with some hills. Tell us about your current riding and we’ll discuss the route options with you. Two guides and a support driver help adapt each day; vehicle-assisted sections are coordinated with the group.

What changes between the two editions?

The shared programme is the same. May is hosted at Casa Pedal & Pause with up to eight guests, home cooking and one restaurant dinner. October uses Bellver Hotel with up to ten guests. Room arrangements and prices differ.

Are airport transfers and lunches included?

No. Flights, airport transfers, lunches, bike rental and travel insurance are separate. Ride snacks and hydration are included. We can discuss arrival arrangements with you before you book.

Can I bring or rent a bike?

Either. Bring a serviced road bike or request one of our premium carbon rental bikes with electronic shifting. Rentals, including Specialized and Cube models, are priced separately and subject to size and availability.

What if I already have a coach?

Keep working with your coach. The preparation plan is included, but you can discuss how to fit the camp into your existing training. Technique sessions and local riding support are available throughout the week.

When is my place confirmed?

A form submission is a booking request. We confirm availability, your total price, payment schedule and cancellation conditions before you pay. Your place is confirmed after your payment has been verified.

What are the payment and cancellation terms?

A deposit secures your booking once availability and payment are confirmed. We confirm the deposit amount before you pay. The remaining balance is due 60 days before arrival.

  • 61 or more days before arrival: the deposit is retained.
  • 31–60 days before arrival: 50% of the total booking price is retained.
  • 0–30 days before arrival: 100% of the total booking price is retained.

These are total cancellation charges, including the deposit, not additional charges. Charges are subject to applicable law and adjusted for costs saved and income recovered from reselling the place.

Replacement guest: for cancellations 0–30 days before arrival, if you or Pedal & Pause find a suitable replacement who takes the same room arrangement and pays the same booking price in full, we refund 100% of your camp payments. An expression of interest alone does not qualify.

Statutory cancellation and refund rights remain unaffected. Contact info@pedalandpause.com to cancel or arrange a replacement.

Can I request a private room?

At Bellver, the supplement is €360 for six nights, subject to availability. At Casa, private occupancy reduces the camp's capacity and is quoted individually. Select your preference and we’ll confirm the options.
