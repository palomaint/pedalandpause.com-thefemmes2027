Is It For You

Who this week is built for.

This is an active cycling week. You should be comfortable riding regularly and climbing on a road bike. Talk to us about your usual distance and pace before booking if you’re unsure whether it fits.

This is your week if

  • You ride regularly and enjoy a mix of coastal roads and climbing
  • You want five days of riding with other women
  • You enjoy a challenge with time to recover afterwards
  • You’re happy to join a small group, on your own or with a friend
  • You like the idea of a small group and a shared table — dinner is together most nights

Probably not your week if

  • You want a solo trip on your own schedule — this is a group week, and the group is the point
  • You’d rather sunbathe than be on the bike five days out of seven
  • You need a fully individual itinerary rather than a hosted group week

Already working with a coach?

You’re welcome. Discuss the camp with your coach and ask us about the planned riding before you book. This is a hosted cycling week; a personal coaching programme is not included.

A day at The Femmes

Most ride days follow roughly the same shape. Nothing is fixed — the group’s pace and the weather set the exact times — but this is the rhythm:

07:30 – 08:30
Breakfast at Hotel Masía Bellver
08:45
Morning ride briefing — route map, key segments, kit for the day
09:00
Ride out
13:00 – 15:00
Ride back, lunch, downtime
15:00 – 19:00
Free time — some afternoons have optional sessions
19:30 / 20:00
Dinner together

Monday breaks this pattern — a shorter ride, a skills-and-recovery session, and the afternoon free for the beach, the hotel pool, or napping. Everyone treats it differently.

Before You Book

The questions we get asked most.

Is the riding right for me?

Tell us your usual distance, climbing and pace. We’ll discuss the proposed week with you. Final route and support arrangements will be confirmed before booking opens; the van is not a substitute for choosing a suitable riding level.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. You can request a room share, or single occupancy for €360 extra, subject to availability. Shared rooms are intended for two riders with separate beds; the exact room configuration will be confirmed before booking.

I already have a coach. Is this for me?

Yes. You don’t need to change coaches. This is a hosted cycling camp, and a personal training plan is not included.

Can I rent a bike?

Yes. We rent premium carbon bikes with electronic shifting, including Specialized, Cube and other brands. Rental is priced separately from the camp; the model and size are subject to availability. Tick the bike-rental box on the form and we’ll send you a quote. You’re also welcome to bring your own serviced road bike.

Are transfers included?

No. Ask for a separate quote, including your airport and travel dates. Transfer availability and the price must be confirmed separately.

How does the private release work?

The list gets 72 hours of first access. The first four paid bookings are €999; remaining places are €1,099 and can be booked by the list as soon as those four are taken. Any places left after the private window open to the public at €1,099. Your price and room choice must be confirmed at checkout before payment.

What happens after I pay?

Your payment provider issues a receipt. Your booking confirmation must show your room, total price, deposit and remaining balance. Departure confirmation is separate; wait for it before making non-refundable travel arrangements.

When is the balance due?

23 August 2027. The balance depends on your booked rate and room choice. Ask us about payment arrangements before booking.
